What is a Benchmark?

A benchmark is a standardized performance test that measures how well computer components (CPU, GPU, and RAM) execute real-world tasks, games, and graphical workloads.

Our benchmarks present frame rate metrics (Average FPS, 1% Lows, and frame time stability) so you can make informed decisions when upgrading hardware or tweaking in-game graphic configurations.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Welcome to HF Plays. We provide real-world hardware benchmarks, gaming optimization tutorials, and PC performance comparisons. We value your digital privacy and strive for complete transparency regarding how your data is handled when you visit our website.

Information We Collect

You can freely browse all benchmark databases and video guides without registration or login. We do not maintain user accounts or collect sensitive data.

Direct Messaging & Contact Form

When using our contact form, no messages are stored on our servers. Depending on your chosen method:

  • Email: Your name, email, and message are formatted into a pre-filled draft in your default email client.
  • WhatsApp: Your inquiry is converted into a secure direct chat link via the official WhatsApp API.

Local Storage & Preferences

We do not use tracking or advertising cookies. We only utilize your browser’s localStorage and sessionStorage to remember your cookie notice choice (Accept / Decline) so the prompt does not reappear during your sessions. You can reset this data anytime via your browser settings.

External Links & Hardware Databases

Our website references external hardware specifications (e.g., TechPowerUp, Intel ARK) and connects to official HF Plays community channels across YouTube, TikTok, Discord, Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn. Please review the independent privacy policies of these third-party platforms when navigating to them.
